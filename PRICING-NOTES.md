# Proposed pricing rationale

These prices were authored for Visionpact Flamingo Media on 15 September 2026 at the owner’s request. They are introductory starting rates for the precisely described scope, not an assertion of established company pricing or market averages. The full rate menu is maintained in app/content.ts and the three packages in app/service-pricing.tsx.

Public reference points consulted:
- [Webcodexa social design](https://webcodexa.com/graphic-design/social-media-post-design) advertises static post design from INR 250.
- [LaunchRoom pricing](https://launchroom.in/pricing) advertises INR 14,999 for a one-page website and INR 24,999 for a five-page package.

These are directional comparisons, not equivalent scopes. The proposed Visionpact rates use INR 399 for a static post, INR 9,999 for a focused landing page and INR 19,999 for a five-page website. Other rates are editorial estimates based on limited deliverable scope, production effort and exclusions, not independently validated market rates. The studio should confirm its actual costs, capacity and margin before making these binding offers.

Packages: Brand essentials from INR 4,999 per project; Social studio from INR 12,999 per month; Website launch from INR 19,999 per project.

Every individual rate specifies its unit and starting scope. One revision round is included unless a package says otherwise. Ad spend, hosting, domains, printing, shoots, stock and AI usage/subscriptions are separately quoted. Applicable taxes, timelines and final scope are confirmed in the quote. No revenue or lead guarantees, fake discounts, popularity claims or payment checkout have been added.

## October 12, 2026: prices raised to 2026 market levels

All 53 service rates and the three packages were raised to sit at the lower end of what Indian freelancers and small studios publish for 2026. That keeps Visionpact competitive while no longer pricing far below the market. Packages: Brand essentials ₹12,999 (was ₹4,999), Social studio ₹19,999 a month (was ₹12,999), Website launch ₹29,999 (was ₹19,999).

Published 2026 ranges used as reference points (directional, not equivalent scopes):
- Websites: small static sites ₹10,000–40,000, business sites ₹40,000–1,50,000, WooCommerce stores ₹25,000–1,00,000 from freelancers; maintenance retainers ₹1,000–5,000 a month ([Hostinger India](https://www.hostinger.com/in/tutorials/website-cost)). Freelancer brochure sites ₹10,000–50,000, agency business sites ₹50,000–2,00,000, custom web apps from ₹2,00,000 ([Fuzen](https://www.fuzen.io/posts/website-development-cost-in-india)). Starter template stores ₹40,000–1,50,000 ([Indian Web](https://indianweb.substack.com/p/e-commerce-website-development-cost)).
- Social media: basic plans ₹5,000–15,000 a month (8–12 posts, 1–2 platforms), growth plans ₹15,000–50,000; single posts ₹500–1,500 ([upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/social-media-packages-india/)). Google Ads management retainers from about ₹20,000 a month at agencies ([upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/google-ads-pricing-india-2026/)).
- Logos: mid-tier freelancers ₹5,000–15,000, boutique studios ₹15,000–50,000 ([Pacewalk](https://pacewalk.com/blog/logo-design-cost-india)).
- Video: basic social edits from about ₹500 a minute, 3-minute corporate explainers ₹15,000–50,000 ([IRPR](https://irpr.agency/blog/video-editing-pricing-pune)).
- Apps: a tightly scoped MVP around ₹3–5 lakh ([Zartek](https://www.zartek.in/app-development-cost-in-india-2026/)).

The studio should still confirm its own costs, capacity and margin. Typical timelines on `/pricing/website-launch` are estimates; each quote confirms the schedule.

## October 12, 2026 (later): owner-set website pricing

At the owner's request, websites are priced from ₹15,000 to ₹50,000 and AI dashboards and business platforms from ₹50,000 to ₹60,000, in round thousands. The Website launch package starts at ₹15,000. The ₹50,000–60,000 platforms (AI dashboard, AI-powered website, business management platform MVP) are below the published ₹2 lakh+ market range for custom web apps, so keep their scope tight: the cards list exactly what is included and what is quoted separately.

Update, same day: at the owner's request websites now start at ₹23,000 (Website launch package and the landing page / portfolio) and still go up to ₹50,000; AI dashboards and platforms are unchanged at ₹50,000–60,000.
